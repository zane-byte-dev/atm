# Code of Conduct

ATM contributors and maintainers are expected to keep participation welcoming, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept reasonable correction.
- Protect private, personal, employer-confidential, and security-sensitive information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. For reports involving security-sensitive information, use a private security advisory. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
